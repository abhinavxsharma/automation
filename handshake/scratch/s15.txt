Model Failure Justification

Explain the specific reasoning error/s that the model made.

The model failed to correctly trace the directed topological critical path of the graph due to multiple crossing edges. Specifically, the planar crossing between directed edges (C, F) and (D, E) caused the model to swap their numerical latency labels: it misassigned the latency of 21 ms (which belongs to edge C -> F) to edge (D, E), which in reality has a latency of only 12 ms. Consequently, in Step 2 of its evaluation, the model calculated the earliest completion time of node E as L(E) = max(44, 40, 36 + 21) = 57 ms instead of the true value max(44, 40, 36 + 12) = 48 ms. Propagating this error forward, it computed L(F) = 57 + 8 = 65 ms and L(T) = 65 + 20 = 85 ms, returning an erroneous final answer of 85 ms instead of the ground truth critical path duration of 76 ms (an 11.8% error).
