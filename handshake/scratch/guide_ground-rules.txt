🚨 Ground Rules
🚫
No LLM Usage

Do not use LLMs to assist in any part of your task creation. Feeding model-generated data into training pipelines actively damages the model. LLM usage is strictly prohibited and will lead to immediate offboarding. We have robust systems to detect this.

🤝
Collaborative Spirit

Everyone here is a team. Don't take a bad review personally. Good data = happy clients = more work for all of us!

🔒
Confidentiality

Everything you learn/read here is strictly confidential. Do not share documents.

⏰
Minimum Commitment

There is no minimum time commitment. However, consistently active and participating fellows are significantly more likely to be invited to other STEM projects as they arise.
