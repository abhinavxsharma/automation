The primary failure mode is a label-to-element mis-binding error. The model correctly identified the graph nodes and edge connectivity, but mis-bound numerical latency badges at the planar crossing of directed edges $(C, F)$ and $(D, E)$. Specifically, it swapped the numerical latency labels, attaching the latency of 21 ms to $(D, E)$ and 12 ms to $(C, F)$. This mis-binding led the model to compute an incorrect critical path latency of 85 ms instead of the ground truth value of 76 ms.
